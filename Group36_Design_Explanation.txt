PROJECT OVERVIEW:
Our project analyzes NYC subway ridership changes from 2020-2024, investigating 
why approximately 1 million daily riders never returned after the pandemic.


VISUALIZATION DESCRIPTIONS:

1. Transit Usage Over Time (mta_usage.html)
   - Shows subway, bus, and bridge/tunnel traffic from April 2020-2024
   - Interactive: toggle between ridership counts and % recovery
   - Key finding: Subways recovered to only 60-80% while cars exceeded pre-pandemic
   
2. Weather Impact (weather_density.html)
   - Violin plots showing how weather affects ridership across pandemic phases
   - Excludes pre-pandemic data (too short period)
   - Key finding: Weather now influences ridership more than before
   
3. Air Quality vs Ridership (aqi_periods_v2.html)
   - Scatter plot with date and AQI sliders
   - Shows ridership vs air quality from April 2020-2024
   - Key finding: Air quality has minimal impact compared to work schedules
   
4. Weekday & Weather Heatmap (heatmap.html)
   - Shows average ridership by day of week and precipitation level
   - Days ordered Monday through Sunday
   - Key finding: Tuesday-Thursday peak shows hybrid work pattern
